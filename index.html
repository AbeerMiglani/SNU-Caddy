    About SNU Caddy

    SNU Caddy shows where each campus caddy is now. A driver shares the caddy’s location only while on shift.

    What the words mean

    Moving
    Driving now.
    Parked
    On shift, and stopped for a minute or more.
    Charging
    Plugged in. Not one to wait for.
    Signal lost
    No update for 90 seconds. The map shows its last place.
    Offline
    The driver is off shift.

    Your privacy

    • Your location stays on this phone. The app uses it to check that you are on campus, to show you on the map and to give the distance to each caddy. It is never sent or saved.
    • Sign-in uses your SNU Google account, only to check that you are from SNU.
    • A caddy’s location is kept only as its newest position. There is no history.
